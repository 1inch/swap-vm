1. SANITY CHECKS - the float model reproduces the XYCConcentrate / v3 algebra
================================================================================================
  L round trip:     1000.000000 -> 1000.000000   (rel err 1.11e-15)
  price round trip: 2000.000000 -> 2000.000000   (rel err 0.00e+00)

  A range symmetric around the current price forces y = P*x, i.e. exactly
  50/50 by value. The only curve with a constant 50/50 split is x*y=const,
  so the self-consistent 'always centred' AMM *is* Uniswap v2 - with the
  whole amplification A showing up inside a single trade only:

    range       y/(P*x)   value in x    A (=virtual/real reserves)
    +/-  1.0%    1.0000      0.5000     201.50x  (measured   201.50x)
    +/- 10.0%    1.0000      0.5000      21.49x  (measured    21.49x)
    +/- 50.0%    1.0000      0.5000       5.45x  (measured     5.45x)
    +/-100.0%    1.0000      0.5000       3.41x  (measured     3.41x)
    +/-300.0%    1.0000      0.5000       2.00x  (measured     2.00x)

  Recentering is a MOMENTUM term. After a buy, the balance ratio y/x moves
  ~A times further than the curve price does, so 'recentre on the current
  price' always pushes the quote further in the direction of the last trade:

    range        A     trade      quote move on curve   extra move from recentring
    +/- 10.0%   21.49   buy 1% of y       +9.31 bps            +8.87 bps
    +/- 50.0%    5.45   buy 1% of y      +36.67 bps           +29.94 bps
    +/-200.0%    2.37   buy 1% of y      +84.35 bps           +48.70 bps

  One application of 'bounds := [spot/k, spot*k]' closes ~1/A of the gap to
  the 50/50 price (theory: dP/P = (1/A) * gap):

    +/- 10.0%  A= 21.49   gap=  0.21280  moved=  0.00987   moved/gap= 0.0464   1/A= 0.0465
    +/- 50.0%  A=  5.45   gap=  0.18232  moved=  0.03339   moved/gap= 0.1831   1/A= 0.1835
    +/-100.0%  A=  3.41   gap=  0.15795  moved=  0.04620   moved/gap= 0.2925   1/A= 0.2929

  The self-consistent rule has NO bounded solution once one side is empty:
  P = y/x -> infinity as x -> 0, so an 'always centred' pool must quote an
  unbounded price when its inventory runs out.

  Inventory-matched recentring: for ANY inventory there is an exact pair of
  bounds of the chosen width whose implied price equals the market price, so
  recentring never requires a swap and never has to jump the quote:

    market drift   value in x   bounds after recentring        implied price error
          +0%         50.0%   [  1818.2,   2200.0]  centre   2000.0      0.00e+00

          -3%         65.6%   [  1818.2,   2200.0]  centre   2000.0      2.22e-16
          +8%         10.0%   [  1818.2,   2200.0]  centre   2000.0      4.44e-16

  the pump unprofitable at every size. The price of safety is the tracking
  speed: the centre can only move (fee/2) per 100% of reserves traded, so
  following a 1% price move needs a turnover of:
    fee=0.050%  ->      40.0x the whole reserve per 1% of price move
    fee=0.300%  ->       6.7x the whole reserve per 1% of price move

3c. COST OF ONE MAKER-TRIGGERED RECENTRING (no swap), range +/-10%, fee 5bps
================================================================================================
  The market drifts, arbitrageurs drag the pool price to the market, the inventory
  ends up skewed, and only then does the maker move the bounds. The 'gift' is what
  arbitrageurs take immediately after the bounds move, % of TVL.

    drift   value in x   symmetric bounds   inventory-matched   real swap to 50/50
       +2%       40.1%         0.087%             0.000%               0.005%
       +5%       25.1%         0.578%             0.000%               0.013%
      +10%        0.3%         2.389%             0.000%               0.025%
      +20%        0.0%         2.416%             0.000%               0.026%
      +50%        0.0%         2.416%             0.000%               0.026%
      -20%      100.0%         1.933%             0.000%               0.020%
      -50%      100.0%         1.208%             0.000%               0.013%

  'real swap to 50/50' includes a 5bps execution cost on the swapped amount.
  Symmetric recentring leaves the implied price ~1/A of the inventory skew away
  from the market, so its gift is ~TVL*skew^2/(8A) - small, but pure loss.
  Inventory-matched recentring costs nothing at all and still needs no swap.
